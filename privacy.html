Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how COLONY LINKS (“we”, “us” or “the service”) handles information when you create an account, build a public profile, or visit a profile hosted on our platform.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: information you provide when registering, such as your name and email address. If you sign in with Google, we receive the basic account information Google shares with us, such as your email address, name and profile image, subject to your Google account permissions.
  • Profile and content: your username, display name, biography, avatar, appearance settings, links and social-media URLs that you choose to add.
  • Uploaded media: images or other supported files you choose to upload for your profile.
  • Technical and usage information: information needed to operate, secure and understand use of the service, such as session data, browser information and profile visits or link interactions.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and manage accounts, display your public profile and links, provide sign-in and account features, store uploaded media, measure basic profile activity, prevent abuse, maintain security and improve the service.

3. Public profiles

Your profile page is designed to be public. Your username, display name, biography, avatar, links and social accounts may be visible to anyone who has your profile link or visits your public page. Do not publish information you want to keep private.

4. Analytics and technical data

To estimate profile visits, the service may generate a daily visitor identifier using a one-way hash derived from technical signals such as an IP address and browser user-agent, together with a service secret. The analytics records store the resulting identifier rather than the raw IP address. These measurements are approximate and may not identify a unique person accurately.

5. Cookies and sessions

We use browser storage and session mechanisms to keep you signed in, remember interface preferences such as language, and protect account features. Disabling these mechanisms may prevent some features from working correctly.

6. Service providers and storage

COLONY LINKS uses third-party infrastructure providers to host the website, process application requests, store account data and store uploaded media. These providers process information as needed to deliver their services and protect the platform. Google sign-in is provided by Google when you choose that option.

7. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information when necessary to operate the service through our infrastructure providers, comply with applicable law, respond to valid legal requests, or protect users and the platform. Information you place on a public profile is intentionally made public by you.

8. Retention and account controls

We keep account and profile information for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain security and meet legal obligations. You can update profile details and links through your dashboard. For account deletion, data-access requests or other privacy questions, contact the COLONY LINKS service operator through the official contact channel provided with the service.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so please use a strong, unique password and keep your sign-in credentials private.

10. Children

COLONY LINKS is not intended for children who are below the minimum age required to use online services in their location. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. The “Last updated” date at the top indicates when the current version was published. Continued use of the service after an update means you have had an opportunity to review the revised policy.

12. Contact

For questions or requests about this Privacy Policy, contact the COLONY LINKS service operator through the official contact channel provided with the service.
